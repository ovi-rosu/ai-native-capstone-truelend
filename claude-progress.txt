the chain instead of re-rendering:
  1. the `## Acceptance Criteria` list in specs/stories/{id}.md
  2. specs/stories/acceptance-criteria.json (the AC index)
  3. features.json -- append, preserving existing `passes` values
  4. node .claude/scripts/test-plan-write.js --force  (matrix + traces; merges
     reviewed required_layers/checks by ac_id and will not overwrite a filled
     test-plan.md unless --reset-plan is also passed)

Check the same hazard before running any *-render-write.js mid-build.
