## User Story
As a portfolio manager, I want one canonical days-past-due ladder so that repayment posting, the end-of-day job and the dashboard never disagree about a loan's bucket.

## Business Value
One classifier means one answer: the posting path, the batch job and the dashboard cannot drift into three different delinquency views.

## Description
Publishes the D3 bucket ladder as an interface-layer artifact: the CURRENT, DPD-30, DPD-60, DPD-90 and NPA enum plus the floor boundaries 0-29, 30-59, 60-89, 90-179 and 180 or more, computed from the oldest unpaid installment's due date. E11-S2 and E12-S1 both consume it.

## Scope
**In:** Delinquency bucket enum with the five FR-09 buckets; Pure classification function over days past due and an as-of date; Boundary tests at every floor
**Out:** must not compute or charge a late fee, penal interest or any lateness charge; must not read or write loan state — the classifier stays pure; must not introduce a sixth bucket or an orthogonal NPA flag, per D3

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E11-S1-AC1** — *Given* the floors CURRENT 0-29, DPD-30 30-59, DPD-60 60-89, DPD-90 90-179 and NPA at 180 or more days past due, *when* a loan whose oldest unpaid installment is 35 days past due is classified, *then* the returned bucket is DPD-30.
- **E11-S1-AC2** — *Given* days-past-due values sampled across 0 through 400, *when* each value is classified, *then* exactly one of the five buckets is returned for every value, with no gap and no overlap.
- **E11-S1-AC3** — *Given* a loan with no installment past its due date as of a given date, *when* it is classified as of that date, *then* the returned bucket is CURRENT.
- **E11-S1-AC4** — *Given* any classification result, *when* it is inspected, *then* it carries a bucket only, with no fee, penal interest or charge of any kind.
