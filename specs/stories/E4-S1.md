## User Story
As a product owner, I want a thin slice from application submission through a returned decision so that the M2 done-when is demoable before intake, scoring and underwriting go deep.

## Business Value
Establishes the submit-score-decide seam early, so the three M2 epics add depth behind a working flow rather than integrating late.

## Description
The D8 tracer bullet for M2. A customer submits against a chosen product, the application is persisted in SUBMITTED state pinned to that product's current policy version per D6, a score is returned, and a decision of AUTO_APPROVE, AUTO_REJECT or MANUAL_REVIEW comes back with at least one reason code.

## Scope
**In:** Application aggregate and submit endpoint; Submission-time policy_version_id pinning; Shallow score and decision response
**Out:** must not implement the full document checklist — that is E4-S2; must not call an external credit bureau or payment rail; must not accept a co-applicant or guarantor on the application

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E4-S1-AC1** — *Given* a chosen product and a CUSTOMER session, *when* an application is submitted, *then* a persisted application in SUBMITTED state is returned carrying the product's current policy version id.
- **E4-S1-AC2** — *Given* that application's age, income and credit-history flags, *when* the scoring stub is called for it, *then* a numeric credit score is returned and stored against the application.
- **E4-S1-AC3** — *Given* the scored application, *when* it is decided, *then* the response carries one of AUTO_APPROVE, AUTO_REJECT or MANUAL_REVIEW together with at least one reason code.
