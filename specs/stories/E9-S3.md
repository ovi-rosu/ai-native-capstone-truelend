## User Story
As a customer, I want a repayment schedule whose installments sum exactly to what I borrowed and owe so that my statement and the bank's ledger can never disagree.

## Business Value
Makes the AC-07 invariants true by construction rather than by tolerance, which is the difference between a reconcilable book and a disputed one.

## Description
Implements the D4 rounding rule: EMI rounded to 2 decimal places, per-period interest from the opening balance, principal as EMI minus interest, and the final installment's principal set to the remaining balance. total_payable is defined as the sum of principal plus interest over the stored rows, not EMI times tenure. The schedule table is append-only per D5.

## Scope
**In:** EMI annuity computation over principal, rate and tenure in fixed-point decimal; Schedule row generation with the final-installment residual rule; Append-only schedule_installment repository and its mutation scan
**Out:** must not define total_payable as EMI multiplied by tenure; must not expose an update or delete method on the schedule_installment repository; must not add a late fee, penal interest or prepayment quote to any installment row

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E9-S3-AC1** — *Given* a generated repayment schedule, *when* the principal column across its rows is summed, *then* the sum equals the disbursed amount exactly, with no residual.
- **E9-S3-AC2** — *Given* that same schedule, *when* total payable is read and the installments are counted, *then* total payable equals the sum of principal plus interest over the stored rows and the installment count equals the product tenure.
- **E9-S3-AC3** — *Given* a schedule whose rounded EMI leaves a residual, *when* the final installment row is inspected, *then* its principal equals the balance remaining after all preceding rows.
- **E9-S3-AC4** — *Given* each period of the schedule, *when* that period's interest is computed, *then* it is derived from the period's opening balance at the rate on the application's pinned policy version.
- **E9-S3-AC5** — *Given* the schedule_installment repository together with src/ and migrations/, *when* they are scanned by the immutability architecture test, *then* no update or delete method is exposed and 0 UPDATE or DELETE statements target schedule_installment.
