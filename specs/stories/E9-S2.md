## User Story
As a product owner, I want a thin slice from approval through disbursement to a posted repayment so that the M3 done-when is demoable before the money epics go deep.

## Business Value
Proves the origination-to-repayment lifecycle end to end at the first M3 story boundary, which is the graded deliverable.

## Description
The D8 tracer bullet for M3. An approved application becomes a loan with a schedule, the loan is disbursed with a released amount and a stub funding source, and a repayment posting lowers outstanding principal. Per D9 it depends only on an approved decision, not on document verification.

## Scope
**In:** Loan aggregate created from an approved decision; Shallow schedule with one row per tenure month; Disbursement and a first repayment posting
**Out:** must not implement the full EMI and residual rules — that is E9-S3; must not call a real payment rail or move real money; must not offer an early settlement, foreclosure or prepayment quote

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E9-S2-AC1** — *Given* an application with an approved decision, *when* its loan is created, *then* a repayment schedule exists with one installment row per month of the product tenure.
- **E9-S2-AC2** — *Given* that loan, *when* it is disbursed, *then* it is in DISBURSED state holding the released amount and a funding-source identifier.
- **E9-S2-AC3** — *Given* the disbursed loan, *when* a repayment is posted against it, *then* the loan's outstanding principal is lower than it was before the posting.
