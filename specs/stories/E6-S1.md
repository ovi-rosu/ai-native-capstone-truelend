## User Story
As an underwriter, I want to work a queue of submitted documents and mark each VERIFIED or REJECTED with a reason so that the applicant knows precisely what to resupply.

## Business Value
Turns document chasing into a worked queue with a recorded reason, cutting repeat rejections.

## Description
The FR-06 verification queue over the checklist items intake generates. Each document transitions to VERIFIED, or to REJECTED with a non-empty reason; actioned documents leave the queue. Runs under the E1-S2 role boundary and is audited as a privileged action.

## Scope
**In:** Verification queue read model over unactioned checklist documents; VERIFIED and REJECTED transitions with a mandatory rejection reason; Queue exclusion of already-actioned documents
**Out:** must not accept a REJECTED transition with an empty reason; must not verify a document against an external registry; must not block schedule generation on document verification, per D9

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E6-S1-AC1** — *Given* a checklist document in the verification queue, *when* an underwriter marks it VERIFIED, *then* its stored status reads VERIFIED.
- **E6-S1-AC2** — *Given* a checklist document in the verification queue, *when* an underwriter marks it REJECTED with a reason, *then* the stored row reads REJECTED and carries that non-empty reason.
- **E6-S1-AC3** — *Given* a document being rejected with an empty reason, *when* the transition is attempted, *then* it is refused and the document's stored status is unchanged.
- **E6-S1-AC4** — *Given* documents that have already been actioned, *when* the verification queue is listed, *then* those documents are absent from the returned queue.
