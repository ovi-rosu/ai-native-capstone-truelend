## User Story
As a customer, I want each repayment to reduce my outstanding principal and refresh my delinquency status so that my standing reflects what I have actually paid.

## Business Value
Keeps the portfolio's delinquency picture accurate the moment money is applied, without a batch lag.

## Description
Implements FR-09 on the D5 mutable/immutable line: the repayment_posting row is appended and never updated, while loan.outstanding_principal and loan.delinquency_bucket are mutable columns updated in the same transaction. Bucket recalculation calls the E11-S1 ladder rather than re-deriving boundaries.

## Scope
**In:** Repayment posting endpoint appending an immutable repayment_posting row; Outstanding-principal reduction by the applied principal portion; Bucket recalculation through the published ladder
**Out:** must not update or delete an existing repayment_posting row; must not charge a late fee, penal interest or open a collections workflow; must not re-derive bucket boundaries locally instead of calling the published ladder

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E11-S2-AC1** — *Given* a disbursed loan with a known outstanding principal, *when* a repayment is posted against it, *then* outstanding principal falls by exactly the applied principal portion of that payment.
- **E11-S2-AC2** — *Given* a posted repayment, *when* the repayment_posting table is read, *then* a new immutable row records the payment and no pre-existing row was updated.
- **E11-S2-AC3** — *Given* a loan whose oldest unpaid installment is 35 days past due, *when* its bucket is recalculated after the posting, *then* the stored delinquency bucket reads DPD-30.
- **E11-S2-AC4** — *Given* a posting that clears every overdue installment, *when* the bucket is recalculated, *then* the stored bucket reads CURRENT and no late fee or penal interest was charged.
