## User Story
As a customer, I want the required-document checklist for the product I chose so that I know exactly what to supply, judged against the rules in force when I applied.

## Business Value
Cuts back-and-forth on missing documents and makes each application's basis reproducible after a policy change.

## Description
Deepens the tracer into real intake: the required-document checklist is derived item for item from the selected product's policy version, and the application stores the policy_version_id resolved at submission. Per D6, a version activated later never changes an in-flight application's basis.

## Scope
**In:** Checklist generation from the product's policy version; Persisted policy_version_id pinned at submission; Checklist item entity with document type and unverified status
**Out:** must not re-resolve an application's policy version after submission; must not verify PAN or Aadhaar against an external registry; must not accept more than one applicant per application

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E4-S2-AC1** — *Given* a selected product with a required-document list on its policy version, *when* an application is submitted against it, *then* the generated checklist matches that policy version's list item for item.
- **E4-S2-AC2** — *Given* a submitted application, *when* it is read back from storage, *then* it is in SUBMITTED state and stores the policy_version_id resolved at submission.
- **E4-S2-AC3** — *Given* a policy version activated after that submission, *when* the application is re-read, *then* its stored policy_version_id and checklist are unchanged.
- **E4-S2-AC4** — *Given* a generated checklist item, *when* it is inspected, *then* it carries its document type and an unverified status.
