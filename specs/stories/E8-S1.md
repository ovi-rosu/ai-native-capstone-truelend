## User Story
As a customer, I want to track my application's status, outstanding documents and decision so that I do not have to be notified or call to find out where it stands.

## Business Value
Replaces outbound notifications with a pull surface, which is why the deny-list can forbid email and SMS entirely.

## Description
The FR-11 tracking view: current application status, every still-unverified checklist item, and — once decided — the decision with its reason codes. This is one of the three screens NFR-10 holds to WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Scope
**In:** Tracking screen showing current application status; List of unverified required documents; Decision and reason codes once the application is decided
**Out:** must not send an email or SMS notification on any state change; must not expose another customer's application; must not show a decision section before the application is decided

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E8-S1-AC1** — *Given* a submitted application belonging to the signed-in customer, *when* its tracking view is opened, *then* the view shows the application's current status.
- **E8-S1-AC2** — *Given* an application with documents still unverified, *when* the tracking view renders, *then* every unverified required document is listed.
- **E8-S1-AC3** — *Given* a decided application, *when* the tracking view renders, *then* the decision and each of its reason codes are shown.
- **E8-S1-AC4** — *Given* an application that has not been decided yet, *when* the tracking view renders, *then* no decision section is shown and no notification was dispatched to the customer.
