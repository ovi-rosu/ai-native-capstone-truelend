## User Story
As an underwriter, I want each application decided against the policy version it was submitted under, with reason codes drawn from that version, so that the basis of every decision is reconstructible.

## Business Value
Gives every approval and rejection a defensible, versioned rationale, which is what makes the policy editor safe to use in production.

## Description
Evaluates the pinned policy version's rules to return AUTO_APPROVE, AUTO_REJECT or MANUAL_REVIEW with at least one reason code, appending a decision_record per D5. Per D6, a MANUAL_REVIEW re-decision and any later override still cite the version pinned at submission.

## Scope
**In:** Rule evaluation producing the three decision outcomes; Reason codes resolved from the application's pinned policy version; Append-only decision_record with latest-record-wins semantics
**Out:** must not update or delete an existing decision_record row; must not return a reason code absent from the application's pinned policy version; must not re-resolve the policy version at decision time

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E5-S2-AC1** — *Given* a scored application, *when* it is decided, *then* the response carries AUTO_APPROVE, AUTO_REJECT or MANUAL_REVIEW plus at least one reason code.
- **E5-S2-AC2** — *Given* the reason codes on that decision, *when* each is looked up, *then* it exists in the policy version pinned on the application.
- **E5-S2-AC3** — *Given* a decided application, *when* the decision is recorded, *then* a new decision_record row is appended and the application's current decision is its latest record.
- **E5-S2-AC4** — *Given* a MANUAL_REVIEW application re-decided after a newer policy version was activated, *when* its decision is read, *then* the re-decision still cites the policy version pinned at submission.
