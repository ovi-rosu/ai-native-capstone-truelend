## User Story
As an operations user, I want disbursement to record exactly what was released and from where so that the loan book reconciles without touching a real payment rail.

## Business Value
Gives the loan book a complete, reconcilable disbursement record while keeping the funding source a stub, as the capstone requires.

## Description
Deepens the tracer's disbursement into the FR-08 record: released amount, funding-source identifier, DISBURSED state with the generated schedule attached, and a guard so a second disbursement attempt cannot double-release. The funding source is a stub per SG-2.

## Scope
**In:** Disbursement transition writing released amount and funding-source identifier; Stub funding-source adapter; Idempotence guard against a repeat disbursement
**Out:** must not call a real payment rail or hold live provider credentials; must not produce a signed loan agreement or e-signature artifact; must not disburse a loan without a generated schedule attached

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E10-S1-AC1** — *Given* an approved loan with a generated schedule, *when* it is disbursed, *then* the loan record holds the released amount.
- **E10-S1-AC2** — *Given* that disbursement, *when* the loan is read back, *then* it holds a funding-source identifier and is in DISBURSED state with its schedule attached.
- **E10-S1-AC3** — *Given* the stub funding source, *when* the disbursement executes, *then* no external payment rail is called and no signed agreement artifact is produced.
- **E10-S1-AC4** — *Given* a loan already in DISBURSED state, *when* disbursement is attempted a second time, *then* the attempt is refused and the recorded released amount is unchanged.
