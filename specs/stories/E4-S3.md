## User Story
As a customer, I want an Apply screen that walks me through product choice and my details so that I can submit an application and immediately see what documents I owe.

## Business Value
Gives the origination funnel its entry point and surfaces the document checklist at the moment of highest attention.

## Description
The customer-facing Apply screen over the E4-S2 intake service: product selection, applicant detail capture, submission, and a confirmation that lists the generated required-document checklist. This is one of the three screens NFR-10 holds to WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Scope
**In:** Apply form with product selection and applicant detail fields; Submission confirmation showing the application state; Checklist display on the confirmation
**Out:** must not add a co-applicant or guarantor field; must not render a native or responsive mobile client shell; must not compute eligibility client-side

## Acceptance Criteria
- **E4-S3-AC1** — *Given* the Apply screen with a product selected, *when* the applicant submits valid details, *then* the confirmation shows the newly created application in SUBMITTED state.
- **E4-S3-AC2** — *Given* the submitted application, *when* the Apply screen renders its confirmation, *then* every required-document checklist item for the chosen product is listed.
- **E4-S3-AC3** — *Given* the Apply form, *when* it is rendered, *then* no co-applicant or guarantor field is present.
