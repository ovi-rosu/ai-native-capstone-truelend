UI-layer story without a mockup

StoryWhy no mockup
E17-S1 It lands the axe-core and keyboard-traversal harness plus the customer-facing screen registry, and remediates the Apply screen. It renders no screen of its own, so inventing one would be scope the story denies.
Scope fidelity. These mockups show only what their stories specify. There is no notification affordance (SG-4), no co-applicant or guarantor field (SG-7), no fee, penalty or collections figure (SG-1), and no early settlement, foreclosure or prepayment quote (SG-10). Interactions demonstrate the flow in local state; none calls a backend.
